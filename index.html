Darren Snake

Snake, service-desk style. Guide Darren round the board collecting mayo and teeth while dodging faulty PCs. Grab four mayo in four seconds for the 23H2 double-points bonus, take on RAM Gigaboo challenges, or go head-to-head in Versus, where big scores dump cat sick on your opponent's board.

Lee Hates Tennis

Arcade tennis where Lee teams up with Ed, Ryan or Will and moans the whole way round. Battle through a World Tour on grass, clay and hard courts, climb a seven-level career ladder, or play a friend on one keyboard or online. He would rather be playing padel.

Tour De Boundy

A ragdoll physics cycling game starring Will Boundy, who never rests. Pedal, hop and flip through seven Devon stages from Devon Lanes to the Torbay Velodrome, swat away a Garmin that keeps begging you to recover, and crash in spectacular fashion.

Nepal Trekk

A wobbly walking sim based on Ryan's real Annapurna Base Camp trek. Pack his bag, then swing one leg at a time up 80 km of the real route over eight days, from Birethanti to base camp at 4,130 m and back down. Overpack and you'll wobble; forget the poles and you'll fall.

Tomato Dave's Ten-pin

3D bowling where the ball is a tomato and Dave has opinions about every throw. Splatter pins across retro alleys, a server room and a disco, unlock new tomatoes and suits for Dave, and get your name on the online leaderboard.

Cuesports

Pool and snooker with realistic ball physics. Play 9-ball, 10-ball, UK 8-ball, snooker, three-cushion and more against the computer or a friend, sharpen up on a free practice table, and work your way through a career from the pub to the arena.
